Sent to me by robots

Really Simple Syndication (RSS) was created in 1999 by a few programmers at NetScape. It was the dominant method of subscribing to multiple websites and continues to be the basis for customized news feeds to this day.

However, RSS is coming to an end. AI makes customization and recommendation infinitely better (in fact, Meta is betting the company on this), and a glorified polling job will not be the future of content syndication.

As such, I have instructed my AI that lives in iMessage to send me great technical blogs whenever it sees one. There is no deterministic polling, the AI just searches and returns based on an inscrutable "taste," a quality that is derived from human-AI collaboration.
